Flight manual

Controls

  • Left stick steers; the ship banks into turns.
  • Right slider is the throttle; hold Boost for a burst.
  • Drag anywhere else to look around the cabin.
  • Tap the cockpit buttons: Space TV, Chromium.iso, Boost, Hyperjump.
  • Dive into any planet except a gas giant to land on its surface. Climb above 1,000 m or tap Orbit to leave.
  • WS or arrows: pitch · AD: turn · QE: roll
  • R/F or wheel: throttle · Shift/Space: boost
  • 1 Space TV · 2 Chromium.iso · C view · J hyperjump · M sound
  • Drag with the mouse to steer; right-drag looks around the cabin.
  • Dive into any planet except a gas giant to land on it. Climb above 1,000 m or press O to return to orbit.
